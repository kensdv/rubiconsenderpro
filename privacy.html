Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. The Short Version

Rubicon Email Suite is self-hosted. Your contacts, campaigns, credentials, templates, and logs live on your machine in an encrypted local database. We cannot see them because they never reach us.

2. What Stays on Your Machine

  • Recipient lists and contact data.
  • Mailbox credentials and tokens — encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM.
  • Campaign content, drafts, and templates.
  • Send logs, bounce records, and analytics.
  • Backups — encrypted, local, yours.

3. What We Actually Collect

  • License validation: your key is checked against our licensing server on activation and periodically thereafter. The check transmits the key itself and a machine identifier — nothing else.
  • Update checks: version pings so the app can offer new builds.
  • Support conversations: whatever you send us on WhatsApp or Telegram when you ask for help.

We collect no analytics, no telemetry, no usage statistics, no email content, no recipient data.

4. What We Never Do

  • We never receive, store, or process your mailing lists.
  • We never see your credentials, messages, or send logs.
  • We never sell, rent, or share your information with third parties.
  • We run no tracking pixels, ad networks, or third-party analytics on this site.

5. Third-Party Services

When you configure a send engine (SMTP providers, Microsoft Graph, Gmail API, AWS SES, and others), your data flows between your machine and that provider under their terms and privacy policies — we are not a party to that relationship.

6. Data Retention

Because operational data never leaves your machine, retention is entirely under your control — delete the database and it is gone. License records are retained to keep your key working.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: WhatsApp or Telegram.
