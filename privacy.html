Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Your Data Stays Yours

Rubicon Email Suite is self-hosted. Your lists, campaigns, credentials, and logs live on your machine in an encrypted local database. They never reach us.

2. What We Collect

Your license key and a machine identifier — checked on activation and periodically to keep your key valid. Whatever you send us when you ask for support. That's all.

3. What We Never Do

No analytics, no telemetry, no tracking. We never see your contacts, your emails, or your credentials. Nothing is sold or shared — there is nothing to sell.

4. Your Providers

When you connect a send engine — SMTP, Graph, Gmail API, SES, others — data flows between your machine and that provider under their policies. We're not part of that.

5. Contact

WhatsApp or Telegram.
