An engine for every door the inbox has

Each engine is its own pipeline — own auth, own error recovery, own personality. If one road closes, you take another.

SMTP RELAY

Any mail server alive — Gmail, Outlook, Zoho, your own. SSL/TLS/STARTTLS with DKIM signing.

DIRECT-TO-MX

Deliver straight to the recipient's MX on port 25. No provider, no toll, no questions.

OPEN RELAY

Auth-free relays and IP-allowlisted hosts — including Google Workspace SMTP relay.

Frequently asked

How do I get access?

Hit GRAB KEY — it opens a WhatsApp chat with the plan pre-filled. Pay by crypto, and your access key + installer come straight back in the chat. Minutes, not days.

What send accounts are supported?

Fifteen methods: SMTP relay (any server), Direct-to-MX, open relay, HTTP/SOCKS proxies, Microsoft Graph (app + delegated), Gmail API, MS refresh-token mailboxes, M365 client-credential tenants, plus SendGrid, AWS SES, Mailgun, Postmark, Brevo, SparkPost, Elastic Email and Mandrill via engine presets — or any HTTPS endpoint via the custom preset. All support rotation, failover, quotas and custom headers.

Where does my data live?

On your machine — contacts, drafts, credentials and logs are stored locally in an encrypted database and never uploaded anywhere. Credentials are AES-256-GCM encrypted at rest.

How does the mailbox pool work?

Import Microsoft RT tokens or app credentials — each mailbox sends as itself through the rotation, with its own daily quota, cooldowns and failover. Dead mailboxes drop out automatically after verification.

What is Rubicon AI?

A built-in AI assistant docked beside every screen. It sees your live sender state — it can rewrite your letter, diagnose failures, verify accounts, pause queues and navigate for you, with a model picker and automatic provider failover.
