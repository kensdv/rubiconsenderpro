Frequently asked

How do I get access?

Hit GRAB KEY — it opens a Telegram chat. Message us which plan you want, pay by crypto, and your access key + installer come straight back in the chat.

What send accounts are supported?

15 methods: SMTP relay (any server), Direct-to-MX, open relay, HTTP/SOCKS proxies, Microsoft Graph (app + delegated), Gmail API, MS refresh-token mailboxes, M365 client-credential tenants, plus SendGrid, AWS SES, Mailgun, Postmark, Brevo, SparkPost, Elastic Email and Mandrill via API presets. All support rotation, failover, quotas and custom headers.

Where does my data live?

On your machine — contacts, drafts, credentials and logs are stored locally in an encrypted database and never uploaded. Credentials are AES-256-GCM encrypted at rest.

How does the mailbox pool work?

Import Microsoft RT tokens or app credentials — each mailbox sends as itself through the rotation, with its own daily quota, cooldowns and failover. Dead mailboxes drop out automatically after verification.

What is RBC Helper?

A built-in AI assistant docked beside every screen. It sees your live sender state — can rewrite your letter, diagnose failures, verify accounts, pause queues and navigate for you, with a model picker and automatic provider failover.
